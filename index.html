01 — THEME & CONCEPT

A diner from the future.

We chose a retro-futuristic theme because it gives every design decision a clear direction: lights, colors, décor, menu, music, packaging and social media.

Why Neon Grill?

Our concept combines the comfort of a classic American diner with a futuristic, digital-first identity. Customers are not only buying food: they are entering an experience built around visual impact, nostalgia and technology.

The theme is specific enough to maintain visual consistency while still allowing us to create promotions, merchandise, an arcade area and social media content.

06 — REFLECTION

What we learned.

01

Organization & Collaboration

The platform helped us divide responsibilities, keep deadlines visible and centralize ideas instead of losing information across different messages and files.

02

Visual Consistency

We learned that repeating the same colors, typography, image style and tone makes a project feel more professional and makes the brand easier to recognize.

03

Digital Citizenship

We promoted respect and responsibility by assigning clear roles, giving constructive feedback, crediting each person's work and making responsible edits to shared content.
